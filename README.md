# NeuroSpartan

## About the project
This is a project made for the Nasa Space Apps Hackathon 

## Title
On-site predictive monitoring of impact on cognitive function, due to radiation exposure, on long term missions on planets without a global magnetic field

## What Makes Our Mission Important
Space is a place that is widely uncharted. With NASA planning to send human astronauts to Mars as early as the 2030s, and no direct way to measure the cognitive impact of radiation along the way, we have to make sure that we can simply monitor and make decisions based on the results of specific tests. Here is where we come in.

## Task
Our mission is to:
1. Create a gaming environment that tracks the cognitive trends of astronauts on missions.
2. Track specific biomarkers that are directly linked to cognitive function such as NfL, GFAP, and $A\beta40$ (research shows that these biomarkers were directly affected during the voyage).

Then, with calculations, we can understand, based on the diagrams’ normalised slope, potential dangers to the rest of the crew (such as passage through a high-radiation patch of space or solar storms).
