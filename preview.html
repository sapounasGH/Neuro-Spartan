Welcome to NeuroSpartan

On-site predictive monitoring of the impact on cognitive function from radiation exposure, on long-term missions to planets without a global magnetic field.

WHAT THIS CONSOLE DOES
  • 01 Neuro Games The crew plays a short daily session; the scores become the cognitive trend line.
  • 02 Mission Data & Sensors Dose, the NFL / GFAP / Aβ40 biomarkers and the suit sensors feed the normalised slope.
  • 03 Earth Manager You choose what is sent back to Earth, and what stays on the habitat server.
NO GLOBAL MAGNETIC FIELD Every trend is measured against that astronaut's own baseline.
